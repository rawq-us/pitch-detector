Beat machine

— steps · — s
Tap the 6×6 pads to audition (top 18 = kit, bottom 18 = an octave up). Click grid cells to program the pattern; Length sets how many bars it spans. With Live record on while previewing, tapping a kit pad writes to the current step.

Master EQ

The master bus is EQ-only (low shelf ~220 Hz · mid peak ~1 kHz · high shelf ~3.8 kHz). Per-layer effects — level, drive, chorus, phaser, delay, reverb, compressor — live on each track: click FX on a track header to open its rack.
