Lyrics

Write in the bracket/paren format — [Singer: C2–G5 style] defines a voice, [Verse 1 - Singer] heads a section, [B2]word sets a note, (parens) = background vocal, [guitar solo] = instrumental. Time is never typed — add it by syncing. The backbone holds time + notes + singers and exports to Apple Music (TTML), karaoke (LRC/SRT), Musixmatch and AI generators.
Section header rule: [Structure - label] — the keyword before the - (space‑dash‑space) is the hard structure (Intro · Verse · Pre‑chorus · Chorus · Bridge · Solo · Outro, optionally numbered); everything after is a freeform label (a singer like Lead / Rapper A, or directions). Singer definitions use a colon: [Lead: C3–A4]. These headers drive the Song structure lane above ↑ (and it drives them back).

Master EQ

The master bus is EQ-only (low shelf ~220 Hz · mid peak ~1 kHz · high shelf ~3.8 kHz). Per-layer effects — level, drive, chorus, phaser, delay, reverb, compressor — live on each track: click FX on a track header to open its rack.
