Guides are bars & beats. Drag clips to move (snaps to the beat). Drag across the ruler to set a cycle region. Click the ⟳ badge on a clip to set how many beats it loops for — or ∞ Fill session to repeat it to the end (a backing track for memo takes). Click a track header to choose where new clips land. The 📝 memo layer records and maps the whole take — chords, notes vs. your mode, a tracked tempo map & lyrics; recording a memo grows the session to fit, then sets its length. Double-click a memo clip (or its ✎ badge) to open the editor & export bundle.

Set the Keyboard to Transpose arp (above the keys), then click a key to jump the selected arp to that offset from C4. Arrow keys: ←/→ ±1 semitone, ↑/↓ ±octave.

Beat machine

— steps · — s
Tap the 6×6 pads to audition (top 18 = kit, bottom 18 = an octave up). Click grid cells to program the pattern; Length sets how many bars it spans. With Live record on while previewing, tapping a kit pad writes to the current step.

Master EQ

The master bus is EQ-only (low shelf ~220 Hz · mid peak ~1 kHz · high shelf ~3.8 kHz). Per-layer effects — level, drive, chorus, phaser, delay, reverb, compressor — live on each track: click FX on a track header to open its rack.
